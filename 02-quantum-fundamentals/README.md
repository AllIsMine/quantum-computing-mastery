# Quantum Fundamentals

This module covers the core principles of quantum computing, building on the mathematical foundations module.

## Planned Topics

- Qubits and quantum states
- Computational basis and superposition
- Single-qubit quantum gates
- Bloch sphere representation
- Quantum measurement
- Multi-qubit systems
- Entanglement
- Quantum teleportation
- Superdense coding
- Density matrices and mixed states

## Learning Method

Each topic will follow the Quantum Computing Mastery learning cycle:

1. Understand
2. Predict
3. Implement
4. Verify
5. Modify
6. Investigate

## Practical Work

Lessons, Jupyter notebooks, Python implementations, exercises, and verification tests will be added progressively as the material is developed and validated.
