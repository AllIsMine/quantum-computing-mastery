# Quantum Hardware Engineering

This module explores the practical engineering considerations involved in executing quantum circuits on simulators and quantum hardware.

## Planned Topics

- Qiskit transpilation and circuit optimization
- Native gates and hardware constraints
- Circuit depth and gate-count analysis
- Quantum noise and noise models
- Simulator-based experiments
- Executing circuits on quantum hardware
- Measurement calibration and readout errors
- Quantum error mitigation
- Benchmarking and randomized benchmarking
- Reproducibility and hardware experiment reporting

## Learning Method

Each topic will follow the Quantum Computing Mastery learning cycle:

1. Understand
2. Predict
3. Implement
4. Verify
5. Modify
6. Investigate

## Practical Work

Experiments will progressively include circuit comparisons, documented configurations, simulator and hardware results where available, statistical analysis, automated checks, and reproducible reports.

Hardware results will be distinguished from ideal simulation results, and limitations will be documented honestly.
