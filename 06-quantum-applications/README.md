# Quantum Applications

This module explores applications of quantum computing, connecting quantum algorithms with optimization, scientific simulation, and machine learning.

## Planned Topics

- Variational quantum eigensolver (VQE)
- Quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA)
- Quantum machine learning (QML)
- Quantum simulation in physics and chemistry
- Hybrid quantum-classical algorithms
- Quantum circuit expressivity and trainability
- Resource estimation and computational costs
- Classical baselines and fair comparisons
- Limitations and potential advantages of quantum approaches

## Learning Method

Each topic will follow the Quantum Computing Mastery learning cycle:

1. Understand
2. Predict
3. Implement
4. Verify
5. Modify
6. Investigate

## Practical Work

Projects will progressively include mathematical explanations, runnable implementations, controlled experiments, classical comparisons, automated tests, and reproducible reports.

Claims about performance or quantum advantage will require appropriate evidence, fair baselines, and explicit discussion of limitations.
