# Quantum Research Laboratory

This module provides a structured environment for conducting reproducible quantum computing research, evaluating published methods, and developing evidence-based research projects.

## Planned Topics

- Research question formulation and hypothesis development
- Quantum computing literature review
- Scientific paper reproduction
- Experimental design and reproducibility
- Research code organization and testing
- Quantum algorithm benchmarking
- Statistical analysis and uncertainty
- Classical baselines and resource comparisons
- Quantum error-correction research experiments
- Quantum machine-learning research experiments
- Research reports, figures, and technical documentation
- Open-source research contributions

## Learning Method

Each research project will follow the Quantum Computing Mastery learning cycle:

1. Understand
2. Predict
3. Implement
4. Verify
5. Modify
6. Investigate

## Research Standards

Research projects should document:

- Research questions and assumptions
- Software versions and experimental configurations
- Methods, datasets, and relevant parameters
- Results, uncertainty, and limitations
- Reproduction instructions and automated tests
- References supporting scientific claims

Conclusions must be supported by evidence. Simulated results must be distinguished from hardware measurements, and limitations must be reported honestly.
