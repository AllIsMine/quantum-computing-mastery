# Benchmarks and Experimental Comparisons

This directory contains reproducible performance measurements and experimental comparisons for quantum computing implementations.

## Planned Contents

- Circuit depth and gate-count comparisons
- Transpilation and optimization comparisons
- Simulator performance measurements
- Quantum algorithm resource estimates
- Quantum error-correction and decoder comparisons
- Quantum machine-learning experiments with classical baselines
- Results tables, plots, and experiment configurations

## Benchmarking Principles

- Define the question and metrics before running experiments.
- Record software versions, configurations, and relevant parameters.
- Use fair comparisons and appropriate baselines.
- Distinguish theoretical estimates, ideal simulations, noisy simulations, and hardware measurements.
- Report uncertainty and limitations where relevant.
- Avoid claiming quantum advantage without sufficient evidence.
