# Research and Experiment Reports

This directory contains structured reports documenting quantum computing experiments, research findings, algorithm evaluations, and reproducible project results.

## Planned Report Types

- Quantum algorithm implementation reports
- Quantum circuit analysis and optimization reports
- Quantum hardware experiment reports
- Quantum error-correction experiments
- Quantum machine-learning investigations
- Classical-versus-quantum comparisons
- Scientific paper reproduction reports
- Research project summaries
- Limitations, lessons learned, and future work

## Reporting Standards

Reports should include, where relevant:

- Research question and objectives
- Theoretical background and predictions
- Methods, configurations, and software versions
- Experimental results and supporting figures
- Statistical analysis and uncertainty
- Comparisons with appropriate baselines
- Limitations and potential sources of error
- Reproduction instructions and references

Claims must be supported by evidence. Clearly distinguish theoretical expectations, simulation results, and measurements obtained from quantum hardware.

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Numbered curriculum modules contain learning activities and project context.
- `src/` contains reusable implementations.
- `tests/` contains automated verification.
- `benchmarks/` contains performance measurements and comparisons.
- `docs/` contains technical instructions.
- `reports/` contains the interpretation and documentation of results.
